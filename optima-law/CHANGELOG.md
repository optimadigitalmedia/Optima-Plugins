# Changelog

## 0.4.5.0 (2026-11-20)

First Optima release, ported from the upstream law-firm tools 0.4.5 (features from 0.1 to 0.4) into Optima's shape: Optima names, the eight-item Optima menu, the shared admin kit and updater, and Optima's Inbox.

- **Disclaimers (Optima → Content → Disclaimers).** The firm's required lines in one place: Not legal advice, Before someone sends a form, Attorney advertising, Prior results, and Reviews and testimonials. Starting wording for North Carolina, Virginia, Florida and Washington, DC, which the firm edits. Louisiana and South Carolina start from the general wording marked `[VERIFY: counsel]`: no state wording is written for them, and a marked line can never be approved until counsel's wording replaces it. A line always prints something: a blank box uses the starting wording. Until a state is saved here, the state saved under Case results is used.
- **Shows everywhere it's set to.** The footer lines render in the theme's `footer` slot (`optima_slot( 'footer' )`), or at the end of the page on a theme without slots, once per page. Under every Gravity Form, next to every case result (through Optima Case results' `optima_results_disclaimer` filter) and under the reviews (Optima Reviews). Shortcodes: `[optima_disclaimers place="footer"]` and `[optima_disclaimer line="relationship"]`. Styles from `assets/optima-law.css` with no color, font or radius of its own.
- **The firm approves the exact wording.** Tick the lines the firm approved and add the attorney's name. The name, date, state and wording are kept together in the approval record (`optima_law_approval_log`). Changing the words later marks the line "Changed since approval" until the firm approves again. Extra spaces don't count as a change.
- **Approval before publishing (Optima → Content → Firm approvals).** Blog posts and People profiles need the firm's approval before they go live; the firm can add pages or other content. Publishing without an approval saves the item as Pending review, from the block editor, the classic editor, quick edit or code. A Firm approval panel in the block editor (sidebar and pre-publish check) and a box in the classic editor take the attorney's name. The name, date and exact saved version (a revision) go in the content log (`optima_law_content_log`). A live item edited after approval stays live and reads "Edited since approval". Case results and reviews keep their own approval.
- **Attorney sign-offs (Optima → Content → Attorney sign-offs).** Lists live pages due for an attorney's review: never reviewed, review out of date, or not reviewed in the review period (6 months by default; 3 or 12). The review itself is Optima Team's Reviewed by record (the page's Reviewed by box, the line at `before-close`, `reviewedBy` and `lastReviewed` in the schema); this plugin builds no second one. The privacy page and pages whose reviewer left (already on the Inbox) are left out. A reviewed page gets Optima Core's WebPage node (`Optima_Schema::add_webpage()`) before Team stamps it, so `reviewedBy` lands on plain pages too.
- **Form entries that delete themselves (Optima → Build → Form entries, operators only). Off by default** until the decision is made (QUESTIONS Q10). When an operator turns it on, Gravity Forms entries older than 90 days (or 30, 60, 180, 365) are deleted each night with their uploaded files, in every status. The first delete waits 14 days after it is turned on; turning it off and on again starts the wait over. Always kept: starred entries, forms that email no one, forms with their own Gravity Forms retention setting, and forms chosen to keep. At most 500 entries per form a night; the deletion record keeps the date and count only. `optima_law_entry_days()` returns the days kept (0 when off), which Optima Core's privacy policy draft reads.
- **Inbox.** `Optima_Law_Inbox::kinds()` returns the Law rows in the shape Optima Build's Inbox uses: disclaimers to approve, waiting for the firm's approval, edited since approval, pages due for an attorney's review, and the form-entry start date while the notice runs.
- **Screens open on what needs doing** (plugin-first rule 52): one status line first, then the waiting items, then the record; settings fold under More options.
- Not ported: upstream's own "Reviewed by" stamp, its review log and its editor panel (Optima Team owns Reviewed by), the Home "Today" list (Optima has no such list; those items are Inbox rows), and the older-kit Policies fallback.
- Tests: `tools/tests/law-disclaimers.php`, `law-approvals.php`, `law-reviews.php`, `law-entries.php`, `law-inbox.php`.
